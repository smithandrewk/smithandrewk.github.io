The Gravity of Old Friends

As we get older, the people we love most scatter across the map. This is a record of the times we still managed to land in the same place.

You don't notice it happening. One by one, the people who used to be a five-minute drive away become a text that says "next time you're in town."

Nobody moves away all at once. The map just quietly stretches until seeing a best friend takes a tank of gas and a free weekend.

So I started keeping track. Not of everyone, just the few that matter most, and the distance it now takes to close. Every dot on the map that follows took a plan, a drive, or a flight. None of them just happened. That's the whole point.
